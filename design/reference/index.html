Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions
How does group buy access work?

Group buy allows multiple users to share premium tool subscriptions at a fraction of the cost. You get full access to all features just like a personal subscription, managed securely by our platform.

What if I can't access a tool?

Try clearing your browser cache and cookies first. If the issue persists, contact our support team with your username and tool name. We typically resolve access issues within 2–4 hours.

Can I upgrade my plan anytime?

Yes! Upgrade anytime from the Shop page. You'll only pay the prorated difference for the remaining period, and new features activate immediately after payment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods: Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Rupay), UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, and popular wallets. All transactions are SSL-secured.

Is there a refund policy?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for new subscribers. Contact support within 7 days for a full refund. After that, refunds are evaluated case-by-case.
